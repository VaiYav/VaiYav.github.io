Product Forge — the framework

My open-source contribution
SpecKit Extension · v1.5.1 · MIT

I built and maintain Product Forge — a full-lifecycle SDLC framework on top of GitHub's Spec Kit.

Standard SpecKit starts from a feature description. Product Forge starts from a problem: validates it (Jobs-To-Be-Done analysis, Go/No-go gate), runs parallel research, builds a product spec through an approval loop, bridges into SpecKit's spec.md, implements with progressive verification, runs multi-agent code review, auto-generates Playwright tests with bug-fix loops, and closes with a post-launch retrospective comparing predicted vs. actual metrics.

The point: every routine engineering decision becomes a deterministic, agent-driven step with full traceability — problem → research → spec → code → tests → metrics. Engineers do the irreducibly hard parts; the agents do the rest.
